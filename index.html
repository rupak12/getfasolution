  • Works with Most Major Student Systems – No need to change platforms. We support Anthology, Colleague, Banner, PowerFAIDS, Regent, Campus Café, and more.
  • Live Customer Support – Real-time assistance all day during working hours—no ticketing queues.
  • Full Compliance Oversight – We ensure audits and program reviews don't become major issues.

2. Financial Aid Staffing

Staffing shortages are costly and disruptive. Our staffing solutions have doubled client revenue from $30K to $60-70K annually:

  • Long-Term, Dependable Staff – No short-term fixes. We provide reliable financial aid professionals.
  • Built-in Redundancy – Our model provides 2-3 professionals per role, ensuring coverage.
  • Flexible Support Models – We integrate seamlessly into your team, whether remote, hybrid, or on-site.

3. Student Outreach & Communication

Colleges struggle with effective student communication. We offer a full-service contact center for:

  • Inbound & Outbound Support – Phone, email, and mass text messaging options.
  • AI-Powered Two-Way Texting – Students prefer texting over calling. We offer real-time messaging.
  • Custom Outreach Programs – Schools can send mass messages to notify students about missing information or deadlines.

Many schools don't know they have compliance risks until it's too late. Our consulting services include:

  • Regulatory & Operational Assessments – Prevent costly audit findings and fines.
