Who We Are

Your Financial Aid Partner

FA Solutions isn't just another financial aid servicer—we're a true partner to the institutions we work with. We integrate seamlessly with schools to enhance efficiency, ensure compliance, and deliver exceptional financial aid experiences. Our team is not only highly experienced but also deeply committed to the success of both institutions and their students.

With a leadership team boasting 25+ years of financial aid experience and a staff of 100-200 full-time W2 financial aid professionals, we have the expertise and scale to support schools of all sizes. Unlike other providers that rely heavily on independent consultants, our full-time team ensures reliability, consistency, and deep institutional knowledge.

What We Believe

Choosing a financial aid partner is a critical decision. Just as schools must carefully evaluate their vendors, we ensure that our partnerships are a strong cultural and operational fit.

We've partnered with nearly 100 institutions over the past decade, offering industry-leading processing turnaround times and hands-on expertise in all major financial aid systems, including Anthology, Colleague, Banner, PowerFAIDS, Regent, and Campus Café.

At FA Solutions, we understand that financial aid is about more than just compliance—it's about ensuring students get the support they need. Our services help institutions streamline operations, reduce administrative burden, and enhance student experiences.
