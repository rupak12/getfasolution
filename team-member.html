Meet Our

Team Member

Brenda Wright

Executive Chief Operating Officer

Brenda Wright is the Founder and Chief Operating Officer at FA Solutions. With over 20 years of executive experience in financial aid operations, Brenda is a trusted leader in the industry. She oversees Financial Aid Processing, Technology, Consulting, Audit, and the Student Communication Center.

Before forming FA Solutions, Brenda served as:

  • COO at University Financial Aid Solutions
  • Assistant VP of Student Financial Services at Saint Leo University
  • Director of Student Financial Services at the State University of New York at Albany

Brenda specializes in compliance, process automation, and leadership within financial aid departments. She holds bachelor’s degrees in Accounting and Economics from Mansfield University (NY) and a master’s in Management from St. John Fisher College (NY).

Brenda is an avid marathon runner, a passionate foodie, and enjoys travel—whether it’s cruising, visiting Napa Valley, or relaxing at the beach.
